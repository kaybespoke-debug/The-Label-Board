How the money moves

No mystery about when you get paid

The portal shows the same numbers we work from. If it says a payment clears on a date, that is the date.

  • Credited on day one. The moment a business you brought first pays, the commission appears in your ledger at the rate it was worked out at.
  • Clears after 31 days. A hold that covers refunds and chargebacks, with the clearing date shown from the start.
  • Paid on the 5th. Once cleared it goes out in that month's run, to the account you nominate.
  • Minimum ₦10,000. Below that it rolls into the next run rather than going out as a transfer barely worth the fee.

The rules, in plain words

Programme terms worth reading

What exactly counts as a referral?

A business that reaches us through your link or code and goes on to pay. Attribution comes from the link, not from anyone claiming it afterwards. If a business was already talking to us before you introduced them, we will tell you rather than quietly count it.

Is it recurring, or only the first payment?

The first payment, once per business. On a yearly plan that is the full year, which is why partners tend to encourage yearly. We would rather pay a good rate once and be clear about it than promise a trickle that gets quietly reduced later.

Why hold it for 31 days?

Because payments can be reversed. The hold means we are never paying out on money that later comes back, which is what keeps the programme solvent and the rates this high.

What if a business I brought cancels?

If they cancel after the hold has cleared, what you were paid is yours. If the first payment itself is reversed inside the hold, that credit is removed, and you will see it in the ledger with the reason.
