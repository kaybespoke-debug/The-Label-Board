The rules, in plain words

Programme terms worth reading

What exactly counts as a referral?

A business that reaches us through your link or code and goes on to pay. Attribution comes from the link, not from anyone claiming it afterwards. If a business was already talking to us before you introduced them, we will tell you rather than quietly count it.

Is it recurring, or only the first payment?

Recurring. Every month a business you brought pays us, you earn a share of that month, for four years from the day they first paid. Years one and two at your rate, years three and four at 3%, and then that business stops earning. Each business has its own clock.

How is my rate decided, and can it go down?

By how many of your businesses are active and paying right now. Under five and nothing is unlocked. Five or more is 6%, fifteen or more is 7%, thirty or more is 8%.

Yes, it can go down. If some of your businesses leave and your count falls, your rate follows it down, from that day forward. If you climb back, it lifts again, also from that day forward. Nothing already credited to you is ever recalculated or taken back, in either direction. A month that was worked out at 8% stays at 8% for ever, whatever happens afterwards.

Why hold it for 31 days?

Because payments can be reversed. The hold means we are never paying out on money that later comes back, which is what keeps the programme solvent.

What if a business I brought cancels?

Its commission stops that day and nothing further is owed on it. What has already cleared is yours. If a payment is reversed inside the hold, that credit is removed and you will see it in the ledger with the reason.

When do I actually get paid?

Commission is credited monthly and paid out once a year, in naira, to a Nigerian account you nominate. The portal shows every month, every business, the rate used and the sum it applied to, so the yearly figure is never a number you have to take on trust.
