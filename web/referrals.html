Questions

The terms, plainly

When does my free month arrive?

Once the business you referred has paid for their first month and that payment has cleared. It shows up as a credit on your next invoice, and we message you to say it has.

Is there a limit?

No cap on how many you can refer. Credits stack, so several referrals in a year can cover several months of yours. If you are referring regularly, the partner programme will be worth more to you than credit.

What if I refer somebody and they only take the trial?

Then nothing is credited, because nothing was paid. Their extra free month is held for them if they come back later and start paying.
