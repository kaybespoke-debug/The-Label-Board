Photography for the website.

WHAT IS HERE NOW: high resolution photographs from Pexels (free for commercial
use, no attribution required), chosen one at a time and checked before use.
They come from different rooms and different photographers, so the stylesheet
puts one treatment over all of them (darkened, desaturated, with a navy wash)
which is what makes them read as one set on this ground. Change that treatment
in site.css under "photographs, once they arrive".

TO REPLACE ANY OF THEM: drop a file in with the same name. Nothing else needs
to change. Keep each one under about 350KB, most phones here are on paid data.

Photographs of your own workroom, your own stock and your own team will always
beat stock, and they are the one thing a competitor cannot copy. When you have
them, this is where they go.

If a file is missing the site does not break: tiles fall back to a gold icon on
a dark panel, and the hero falls back to a gradient.
