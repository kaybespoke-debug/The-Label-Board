The product

Everything your business runs on, in one app

Most software for this trade stops at a list of orders. The part that decides whether a business survives growing is the part underneath: stock that comes off the shelf, wages that follow the work, and books you could hand to a bank.

Back to the overview

An order that carries everything about itself

Made to order or sold over the counter, both live here, with the customer, the money and the measurements on one record rather than in three books.

  • Measurements kept per customer, with every piece you have made for them.
  • Deposits and balances to the naira, and what is overdue surfaced without you looking.
  • Fittings and due dates on one calendar rather than in four different places.
  • Invoices, receipts and job sheets on your own letterhead.

A board that answers the only question that matters today

What is late, what is close, and who is holding it. The stages are yours to name, whatever the bench actually calls them, and a piece is not finished until somebody has signed for it at the other end.

  • Late work is loud, colour coded rather than buried in a report.
  • Every maker sees their own pile when they open the app.
  • The customer hears from you when the stage moves, in a message that is ready to send.
  • Dispatch and delivery on the same record, so what has left the workroom is still accounted for.

The shelf and the screen agree

Cloth by the yard, shoes by the pair, buttons by the card, ready to wear by the size. Count it in whatever unit you actually buy and sell in.

  • It comes off the count when a job uses it or a customer takes it.
  • Low stock warnings before you find out at the cutting table.
  • What a job ate in materials shows up in what that job made.
  • Vendors and suppliers with what you paid and what you still owe.

Real books, and pay that follows the work

Every order, wage, roll of fabric and expense lands in the same ledger, so the profit figure is arrived at rather than guessed.

Profit per order

What a job actually made, after everything it cost you. You see which work is worth taking again.

Money in, by where it came from

The studio, Instagram and WhatsApp on separate lines rather than one total you cannot explain.

Pay worked out for you

Set each person up once and what they are owed comes from the jobs they finished, with payslips at the end of it.

Rota, attendance and leave

Hours recorded rather than remembered, and leave counted against a balance instead of an argument.

Branches

Each outlet with its own staff, stock and takings, rolled up when you want the whole picture.

Reports you can hand over

Period by period, branch by branch. What an accountant or a lender asks for.

It does not stop when the network does

Install it from the browser and it sits on your home screen like any other app. With no signal it still opens, still saves and still prints. When you are back it catches up on its own.

  • Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac, with nothing to download from a store.
  • A dropped line never costs you an order.
  • Built for a phone first, not a desktop page squeezed onto one.
  • Who did what, and who is allowed to. Roles that fit a real workroom, and a record of every change.
  • Your data stays yours. Everything exports, any day you ask, in a file you can open anywhere.

Built for a real Lagos day

Power goes. Data finishes. A customer is standing in front of you and the page will not load. Everything here assumes that happens and keeps going anyway, because software that only works on a good day is not software a business can lean on.

Not only for tailors

Tell it what kind of business you are, and it changes

A bespoke tailor, a shoe maker, a bag maker, a fabric shop, an accessories bench, ready to wear garments off the rail, or more than one of those at once. You pick when you set up, and again for each outlet. What you never use stops taking up room.
