Questions and answers

The things people actually ask

The software and your records, the first month of using it, and how the partner programme pays. If yours is not here, ask us and we will answer it properly.

Straight answers

The software, and your records

What it does on a bad line, where your data sits, and how you get it out again.

Does it work when the network goes?

Yes. Everything saves on your device first and catches up when you are back, so a dropped line never costs you an order. Software that only works on a good day is not software a business can lean on.

Where is my data kept?

On your own device first, and in an encrypted cloud database when you are signed in with an account, so it can move between your phone and the shop laptop. Each business's records are separated at the database level, which means one business cannot see another's, ever.

Can I take my records out again?

Whenever you want, in plain files you can open anywhere. We will never hold your customer list hostage to a subscription, and we will never sell what is in your books.

Why is it priced in Naira?

Because it is for the size of business that actually exists in this market, rather than converted from a dollar price sheet written for a shop in another country.

Who will I actually be talking to?

A small team. We are taking businesses on carefully rather than as fast as possible, so when you book a walkthrough in these early months it is likely to be the founder on the other end of it.

What happens if you disappear?

A fair question to ask any young software company. Your data exports in full at any time, and the app keeps working on a device it is already installed on. We are building something we intend to run for a long time, and we would rather you had that safety net anyway.

Billing questions

The things worth knowing before you pay

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or card, monthly or yearly. We will send an invoice with the account details, and a receipt once it lands. Talk to us on WhatsApp if you want to pay in a way that suits your books better.

Can I see it working before I pay?

That is what the walkthrough is for. We put your own orders, stock and staff into an account with you on the call, so what you are judging is your business rather than a made up one. If you want to go on from there we agree a plan and send an invoice. Nothing is charged automatically, because we never take a card.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, in either direction. Moving up takes effect straight away and we only bill the difference. Moving down takes effect at your next renewal so you keep what you have already paid for.

What counts as a team seat?

Anyone who signs in with their own name. A maker who only looks at their own work still uses a seat, because the record of who did what depends on people signing in as themselves.

Do you charge per order or take a percentage?

No, and we do not intend to for the subscription. If we ever add paid extras, such as collecting card payments on your behalf, the cost will be stated plainly before you switch it on.

What if I stop paying?

The app stops opening new work, and your data remains exportable in full. We do not delete a business's records without telling you well in advance, and we never sell them.

Is there a discount for an association or a group of shops?

Yes. If you are bringing several businesses on together, talk to us. There is also a partner programme that pays you for it.

Common questions

The first month

Getting your old records in, who on your team can see what, and what to do when something looks wrong.

How do I get my old records in?

There is an import for customers, orders, stock, suppliers, staff and past payments, and it reads an ordinary spreadsheet. Running it twice does not create everything twice, so you can fix a messy file and import again. Send us the file and we will do the first one with you.

My staff can see things I do not want them to see.

Roles control that, and you can change them yourself in Settings. Finance, payroll, expenses and funds sit behind a lock. If somebody is seeing something they should not, it is a role setting rather than a limitation, and we will walk you through it.

It is not syncing between my phone and the shop laptop.

Sync happens when you are signed in with an email address and online. A username sign in stays on that one device by design, which is what makes the offline mode work. If you are signed in properly and still not seeing changes, message us and we will look at the account rather than guess.

I think I have lost something.

Tell us straight away rather than trying to fix it. There is an audit trail of who changed what and when, and the sooner we look the more likely it is that we can put it back exactly as it was.

Can you build something we need?

Ask. A good part of what is in the app came from somebody saying this would be easier if it did that. We will tell you honestly whether it is coming, whether it is a maybe, or whether it is not something we will build.

The rules, in plain words

The partner programme

What counts as a referral, how the eight per cent is worked out, and when it reaches your account.

What exactly counts as a referral?

A business that reaches us through your link or code and goes on to pay. Attribution comes from the link, not from anyone claiming it afterwards. If a business was already talking to us before you introduced them, we will tell you rather than quietly count it.

Is it recurring, or only the first payment?

It depends how that business pays us. On a monthly plan you earn 8% of every monthly payment for twelve months from the day they first pay, and then that business stops earning. On a yearly plan you earn 8% of that year’s payment once, because there is one payment to take a share of. Each business has its own clock, counted from its own first payment.

Is the rate the same for every partner?

Yes. 8% from your first business, whether you have brought one or fifty. There is no ladder to climb, no rate to unlock and nothing to fall back down. A partner with two businesses and a partner with forty are paid at the same rate on each one.

It also means your rate cannot move under you. What changes month to month is how many of your businesses are still paying, not what each of them is worth to you.

Does the business I refer get anything?

No, and that is deliberate. They pay the ordinary price on the ordinary terms, and the commission comes out of our side rather than theirs. It means you are never introducing us on the strength of a discount we might one day stop offering, and it means what you earn does not depend on what they were promised.

Why hold it for 31 days?

Because payments can be reversed. The hold means we are never paying out on money that later comes back, which is what keeps the programme solvent.

What if a business I brought cancels?

Its commission stops that day and nothing further is owed on it. What has already cleared is yours. If a payment is reversed inside the hold, that credit is removed and you will see it in the ledger with the reason.

When do I actually get paid?

Commission is credited monthly and paid out once a year, in naira, to a Nigerian account you nominate. The portal shows every month, every business, the rate used and the sum it applied to, so the yearly figure is never a number you have to take on trust.

Ask a person. We would rather answer it than have you guess.
