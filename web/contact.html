Getting started

Your first week, in the order that works

Businesses that do it in this order are running properly within a week. Businesses that try to enter three years of history first usually stall, so we ask you not to.

Put your own name on it

Company name, logo, address and bank details in Settings. Every invoice and receipt from then on carries them.

Enter only what is open

The jobs still in the workroom and the balances still owed. Not history. That is usually an evening, and it is the whole migration for most businesses.

Add the people

Your team, their roles and how each one is paid. The rota, attendance and payroll screens appear once there are two of you.

Count the shelf once

An opening stock count, in whatever units you buy in. From there it keeps itself, because sales take stock down.

Run one job all the way through

Take it from order to fitting to paid, and watch the books move. That is the moment it stops being software and starts being how you work.

Common questions

Things people ask in the first month

How do I get my old records in?

There is an import for customers, orders, stock, suppliers, staff and past payments, and it reads an ordinary spreadsheet. Running it twice does not create everything twice, so you can fix a messy file and import again. Send us the file and we will do the first one with you.

My staff can see things I do not want them to see.

Roles control that, and you can change them yourself in Settings. Finance, payroll, expenses and funds sit behind a lock. If somebody is seeing something they should not, it is a role setting rather than a limitation, and we will walk you through it.

It is not syncing between my phone and the shop laptop.

Sync happens when you are signed in with an email address and online. A username sign in stays on that one device by design, which is what makes the offline mode work. If you are signed in properly and still not seeing changes, message us and we will look at the account rather than guess.

I think I have lost something.

Tell us straight away rather than trying to fix it. There is an audit trail of who changed what and when, and the sooner we look the more likely it is that we can put it back exactly as it was.

Can you build something we need?

Ask. A good part of what is in the app came from somebody saying this would be easier if it did that. We will tell you honestly whether it is coming, whether it is a maybe, or whether it is not something we will build.
