About us

We built this for ourselves first

The Label Board came out of a working fashion label in Lagos. Every screen in it exists because a real studio needed it on a real day, and nothing in it was designed by somebody who has never had a customer standing in front of them asking where their dress is.

The story

Ten years in it, and four of them a thousand miles away

I have spent ten years in this trade. I know what it is to take a measurement, to price a job badly and find out three weeks later, to promise a Saturday and be cutting on the Friday night.

Then for four of those years I was running the business from another country. That is when it stopped being about being busy and started being about whether anything was actually written down. I would ask how a job was going and get an answer that was true for whoever was holding the phone. I would ask what we made last month and nobody could tell me, because the answer was in a notebook, a WhatsApp thread, a stack of bank alerts and somebody's memory, and none of the four agreed.

Nothing was being stolen. Nothing was being done badly. There was just no system, and without one a studio cannot see itself. You feel the work. You do not see the business.

So I tried the software that exists. Some of it was built for boutiques in other countries and priced for them. Some of it tracked orders nicely and knew nothing about what sits underneath one. None of it knew what a piece rate is, or that the network goes, or that the fabric a job used has to come off the shelf and off the profit in the same breath.

This is the thing I needed. It has been used in my own studio every day while it was being built, and the parts that were annoying were fixed because I was the one being annoyed by them. It is not another piece of SaaS looking for an industry. It is ten years of this one, written down.

Why it is called The Label Board

A label is the business. The board is the thing on the wall of every workroom that tells you what is being cut, what is being sewn and what has to go out today.

I wanted software that felt like that board, in your pocket, and honest about the money underneath it. Something I could open in another country and know what was really happening in the workroom.

What we believe

Four things we will not compromise on

It has to work with no network

Software that only works on a good day is not software a business can lean on. Everything here saves on your device first and catches up later, because that is the reality of the day you are actually having.

Your data belongs to you

Export everything, whenever you want, in plain files you can open anywhere. We will never hold your customer list hostage to a subscription, and we will never sell what is in your books.

We do not take a cut of your sales

One fee for the business. Your customers pay you, into your own account. We are not standing in the middle of your money, and a busy month does not cost you more.

Priced for businesses here

In Naira, for the size of business that actually exists in this market, not converted from a dollar price sheet written for a shop in another country.

Where we are

Small on purpose, for now

We are taking businesses on carefully rather than as fast as possible. Every one of the first gets us on a call, gets their data brought in with them, and gets the founder's attention when something is wrong.

That is not a growth strategy. It is how you make sure the thing actually works in a hundred different workrooms before it is in a thousand.

Straight answers

The questions people ask about us

Who is behind this?

A small team in Lagos, led by a founder who runs a bespoke label and got tired of running it out of a notebook. When you book a walkthrough, that is who you are likely to be talking to.

Where is my data kept?

On your own device first, and in an encrypted cloud database when you are signed in with an account, so it can move between your phone and the shop laptop. Each business's records are separated at the database level, which means one business cannot see another's, ever.

What happens if you disappear?

A fair question to ask any young software company. Your data exports in full at any time, and the app keeps working on a device it is already installed on. We are building something we intend to run for a long time, and we would rather you had that safety net anyway.
