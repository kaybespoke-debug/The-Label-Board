Billing questions

The things worth knowing before you pay

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or card, monthly or yearly. We will send an invoice with the account details, and a receipt once it lands. Talk to us on WhatsApp if you want to pay in a way that suits your books better.

What happens at the end of the free 14 days?

Nothing is charged automatically, because we never took a card. We will ask which plan you want. If you decide not to continue, your data stays available to export.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, in either direction. Moving up takes effect straight away and we only bill the difference. Moving down takes effect at your next renewal so you keep what you have already paid for.

What counts as a team seat?

Anyone who signs in with their own name. A maker who only looks at their own work still uses a seat, because the record of who did what depends on people signing in as themselves.

Do you charge per order or take a percentage?

No, and we do not intend to for the subscription. If we ever add paid extras, such as collecting card payments on your behalf, the cost will be stated plainly before you switch it on.

What if I stop paying?

The app stops opening new work, and your data remains exportable in full. We do not delete a business's records without telling you well in advance, and we never sell them.

Is there a discount for an association or a group of shops?

Yes. If you are bringing several businesses on together, talk to us. There is also a partner programme that pays you for it.
